Research Interests

  • Quarkonia production at intermediate energies (SPD/NICA)
  • Hadron structure and higher-twist partonic distributions: PDFs, GPDs, TMDs, GTMDs
  • Light-front models for non-perturbative QCD
  • Advanced computational physics: Python, Mathematica, LaTeX, C++, Fortran
